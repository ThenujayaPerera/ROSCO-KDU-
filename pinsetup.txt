Here is the wiring breakdown for connecting your two GA25-370 motors to the TB6612FNG motor driver board shown in the image.
To keep code logic consistent across both sides (so forward on Motor A moves forward on Motor B), assign Channel A to the Left Motor and Channel B to the Right Motor.
Left Motor Wiring (Channel A)
Motor Wire Color	Wire Function	Driver Pin	Microcontroller / Power
Red	Motor Power (+)	AO1	—
White	Motor Power (-)	AO2	—
Blue	Encoder Power (+)	—	3.3V / 5V Logic VCC
Black	Encoder Power (-)	—	GND
Yellow	Encoder Signal A	—	GPIO 41 (PIN_ENC1_A)
Green	Encoder Signal B	—	GPIO 42 (PIN_ENC1_B)
	Driver Logic Control for Left Motor:
	PWMA: Connect to a PWM-capable GPIO for Left Speed.
	AIN1 / AIN2: Connect to GPIO pins for Left Direction control.
Right Motor Wiring (Channel B)
Note on motor direction: Because the right motor is mounted symmetrically opposite to the left motor, its polarity (Red/White) is swapped relative to the driver outputs. This ensures that sending the same direction signals (IN1 = HIGH, IN2 = LOW) to both channels drives the robot straight forward.

	PWMB: Connect to a PWM-capable GPIO for Right Speed.
	BIN1 / BIN2: Connect to GPIO pins for Right Direction control.
